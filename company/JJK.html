JUJUTSU KAISEN MODULO

In a future where the boundary between human civilization and the supernatural has fractured, traditional Jujutsu fails against threats descending from beyond the stars. A new generation of sorcerers must master Jujutsu—the volatile cursed energy left behind by the legends of the past. Jujutsu Kaisen Modulo follows Yuka and Tsurugi Okkotsu, descendants of the strongest bloodlines, who are thrust into a celestial conflict after unsealing a relic that connects them to a forgotten era. In a world of Neo-Tokyo neon and cosmic horror, they must navigate a landscape where Cursed Techniques have evolved into planetary threats. Expect reality-bending expansions, a ruthless new hierarchy of power, and a narrative that questions the very cost of humanity. This is a journey where the past is a weapon, the future is a curse, and the math of survival always ends in blood.
